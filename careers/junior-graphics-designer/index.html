Junior Graphics Designer

Designs Remote Full-Time Mid Level

About the Role

We are looking for a creative and detail-oriented Designer to join our growing team. You will be responsible for creating visually compelling designs that communicate our brand, products, and ideas across digital platforms. The ideal candidate has a strong design sense, can work collaboratively, and is passionate about delivering high-quality visual experiences.

Requirements

  • Proven experience as a Designer or similar role
  • Strong portfolio showcasing creative and professional design work
  • Proficiency in design tools such as Figma, Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, or similar
  • Solid understanding of layout, typography, color theory, and branding
  • Ability to turn ideas and concepts into visually appealing designs
  • Strong attention to detail and creativity
  • Ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines
  • Good communication and collaboration skills
  • Willingness to learn, adapt, and improve continuously

Responsibilities

  • Create visually engaging designs for digital and marketing materials
  • Develop and maintain consistent brand visuals across all platforms
  • Collaborate with marketing, product, and development teams
  • Translate ideas, concepts, and briefs into high-quality designs
  • Design graphics for social media, websites, and promotional campaigns
  • Revise and improve designs based on feedback
  • Ensure designs meet brand guidelines and project goals
  • Manage multiple design tasks and meet deadlines
  • Stay updated with design trends and tools

  • Career growth and skill development opportunities
  • Flexible work environment
  • Hands-on experience with exciting projects and brands
  • Supportive and collaborative team culture
  • Opportunity to make real impact and contribute ideas
  • Access to modern tools and resources
